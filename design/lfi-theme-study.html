01

Typeface — Archivo

Settled

Acumin is a Slimbach neo-grotesque in the Franklin / American-gothic line: tall x-height, moderate stroke contrast, fairly closed apertures, and a full width axis. Archivo shares that lineage and — unlike most substitutes — carries a real variable wdth axis from 62 to 125, so the condensed uppercase treatments the guide calls for come from the same family rather than a separate condensed face.

It is also SIL Open Font License, so it can be bundled inside the .app with no per-seat licence. That retires the blocker from the first pass: the theme no longer has to fall back to the system face and lose its condensed treatment. Everything on this page is set in it.

Corner radius — 8px

Settled

The same step card and status chip at each candidate radius. The guide specifies 2px — "restrained corner rounding" — while shotAI ships 10–12px today. This is the change most likely to provoke a reaction, and it is independent of the palette.

At 22px the numbered badge is the tell: by 10px it is nearly a circle and reads as a consumer app, at 2px it reads as a printed spec sheet. The step card itself changes far less. 8px is the choice — visibly crisper than today, without turning the badge into a square. It is a deliberate departure from the guide's 2px: the corporate palette lands, the corporate geometry is softened to suit an app rather than printed collateral. 03

Success green — forest

Settled

LFI defines no green, and the olive from its chart ramp read as ambiguous for success. A forest green sits far enough from rust to be unmistakable while staying warm rather than going teal. It also measures better: 6.8:1 against its own tint versus the olive's 5.6:1.

06

Decided

Typeface — Archivo

Same gothic lineage as Acumin, with a variable width axis so condensed comes from one family. OFL, so it bundles with the app and the fallback problem disappears.

Radius — 8px

Chosen over the guide's 2px. The badge is where the difference reads; the card barely moves. 8px keeps the corporate palette while staying an app rather than a spec sheet.

Green — forest

#3E7D5A replaces the chart olive. Unambiguous as success, still warm, and measurably higher contrast on its own tint in both appearances.

Capture colours — unchanged

The recording pill, area-select overlay and marker reds stay hardcoded and theme-agnostic, matching Windows. Consequence: during a recording the violet selection rectangle still appears, so the corporate theme is not end-to-end.

Mock-up only — nothing in shotAI/Theme.swift has been changed. The app surfaces are hand-built approximations at roughly 70% scale, accurate to the current layout but not pixel-measured against a running build. Both appearances are shown side by side regardless of your own light/dark setting, since a theme has to be judged in both.
